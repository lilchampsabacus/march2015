Common questions

Everything a parent needs to know before starting.

Do you offer a free trial class?

Yes. Parents can book one trial class to understand the teaching approach before enrolling.

How often are regular classes?

Regular classes are held once a week for approximately two hours.

Is website practice included?

Yes. Enrolled students receive access to level-based website practice without an additional practice charge.

Can students practise on mobile?

Yes. The practice website is designed for mobile, tablet and computer use.

Do students receive competition preparation?

Yes. Timed practice, speed work and accuracy correction are part of competition preparation.

Which Ambernath locations are available?
